# Skene example
#
# One place, one run: a store, two workers, a machine, a sensor, a rover, a camera and a reader.
# It carries three chains, which is what a queue can be in: one closed in the fact it produced, one
# taken and not yet concluded, and one nobody has taken.
#
#   ktema-sim scenario/example.txt /tmp/example-volume --reset

seed 7

tick 0
position blackboard 320,180
position worker-1 150,90
position worker-2 150,270
position machine-3 320,70
position sensor-1 80,180
position rover-1 470,90
position camera-1 470,270
position reader-1 600,190
count 8

# The reading the work starts from, and what bounded the judgement.
fact reading-1 48 sensor-1/observation
hint bound ceiling=one,floor=zero
count 9

# The declaration, the taker and the outcome.
intent answer reading-1 take-the-work
claim answer worker-1
fact answered 48 worker-1/result
count 10
conclude answer work-done
count 11

# A second piece of work, taken and not yet concluded.
intent inspect answered check-the-tolerance
claim inspect machine-3

# A third, declared over the camera's reading, that nobody has taken.
tick 900
fact reading-2 48 camera-1/observation
intent audit reading-2 write-the-report
count 12

# The rover crosses the yard, which is the trail the map draws rather than a jump.
tick 30
path rover-1 470,90 500,250 6 30

# A worker picks up the work nobody had taken.
tick 300
position worker-2 200,300
count 19

flush
